The Wellington Ruby on Rails group (WellRailed) is meeting together on April 25th for a day of hanging out, hacking on code, and helping new people get started with Rails.

It's a bit like a mini rails camp and is going to be pretty free-form. There will be pizza. There will be a "boot up" corner where people who are new to Rails can get help installing it on their computer and running through the basics. But most importantly, there will be a room full of people who love web technology and are keen to be a part of the community.

Come along! And bring a laptop if you can. =)
