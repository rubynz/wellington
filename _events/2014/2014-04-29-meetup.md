6 lightning talks about gems which the presenters have as part of their standard set but are not widely known of.

We still have spots for a few more talks so if you have a fav gem that you think everyone should know about please contact either Nahum or Matt. Lightning talks are only 5-10 minutes each and are a great way to have a go at public speaking / presenting if you've never done it before. At the beginning of the meeting we'll have time for announcements from business looking for staff, people looking for work, newly released products, libraries etc... and mention of relevant upcoming events.
