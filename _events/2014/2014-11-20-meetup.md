We're back again for another meetup!

Mal Curtis (https://twitter.com/snikchnz)
Escaping Heroku Postgres: Sometimes decisions come back to bite you, and using Heroku Postgres was one of those. Our data was stuck in a black box and we needed it out. Learn how Apostle.io (http://apostle.io) migrated off Heroku's Postgres platform to RDS without disrupting their service, or losing any data.

Samson Ootoovak (https://twitter.com/ootoovak)
Micro App Deploys: Do you like Heroku deploys but don't like the lock-in? Do you have many small projects you want to easy easily share with the world? Does being able to SSH into a familiar Linux environment just make you feel all warm and fuzzy inside? If so come listen to an expert (see: someone who has used Dokku once) talk about Dokku and why it is a tinkerers dream!
