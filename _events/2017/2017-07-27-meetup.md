So far we have 2 speakers lined up:

First up with have Regan Ryan giving an introduction to Ruby Meta Programming — learn the nuts and bolts of how Ruby works, and begin the meta-programming journey.

Then we have Mathew Hartley giving a demonstration and introduction of Amazon's IoT, as well as some of the big data tools like Athena and Quicksight. The goal is to show a few of the (quite interesting) tools you wouldn't be using in production, in the context of recording house temperatures.

Doors open at 5:30pm with talks starting at 6:00pm. We'll break for pizza and chatting/networking after the 1st talk.
