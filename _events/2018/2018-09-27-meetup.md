Important: please note these meetings are now regularly on the 4th Thursday of each month and we're seeking speakers for the next 6 events. Further details have been pinned in the #wellington channel of the Ruby NZ Slack (http://slack.ruby.nz/).

This month we have a talk by Cara and Dan from Rabid:

Cara and Dan are Ruby Association Certified Ruby Programmers Silver version 2.1. What is that, you ask? We'll tell you all about it - why we did it, what it involved, and whether or not we'd recommend anyone else do to it.

Please get in touch if you'd like to present something, we've had a wide variety of topics and presenters in the past, so don't feel shy!

Doors open at 5:30pm with talks starting at 6:00pm. We'll break for pizza and chatting/networking after the 1st talk.

Please join us in the Ruby NZ Slack

Lots of us are hanging out there, you can get an invite from the official Ruby NZ (http://ruby.nz/) site here (http://slack.ruby.nz/). Don't forgot to join the #wellington channel too as that's were any discussion and announcement about this meetup occurs.

Sponsorship

Optimal Workshop (https://www.optimalworkshop.com) is once again sharing their sweet office with us and covering the pizza. They're also a big fan and sponsor of the NZ Rails Camps!! Thanks!
