This month we have Raquel Moss talking about Purchasing Power Parity:

"If you market your services globally, you might be missing out on customers who can't afford your product because their currency doesn't stack up favourably against yours. Let's take a dive into Purchasing Power Parity--we'll explore what it is, why you might want to introduce it into your app, and how I implemented it for an egghead.io project.

The talk will comprise three sections. First, I'll give an explanation of Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) for those of us who aren't economists. We'll talk about the Big Mac Index, which people might be familiar with. Next I'll talk about how PPP is calculated, to give people an idea of how they might implement it in their apps. Finally, I'll dig into how I implemented PPP for an egghead.io project (testingjavascript.com), a Gatsby site that communicates with a Ruby on Rails back-end."
