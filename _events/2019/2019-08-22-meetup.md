Come check out the new breakout space that Flick has expanded to, and hear about some new features that the Flick Tech Team has been working on! There'll be a range of Garage Project beers on offer and plenty of non-alcoholic options too 🍹🥂🥛

**New Join Form 📄**

Team FEDUP (Front End Design, User experience & Product) reworked the join form to use the pattern library and a/b tested it against the old one. This allowed us to use new web form conventions and improve our conversion rates!

**Self Serve Payments 💰**

A proportion of Flick customers miss paying their bill every week, and get in touch with the customer support team and sort out a manual solution. We built an integration with Windcave (formerly DPS) to support customers doing this themselves. Come find out how many microservices we needed to modify to build this!
