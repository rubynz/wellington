This September, Flick Electric's crew will open their doors and will talk us through their engineering stack and how they solved some engineering challenges specific to the Electricity industry.

## Talks

Eoin Kelly, lead architect at Ackama, is our guest speaker and will be talking about advanced SQL Queries in Rails.

Elspeth Dick, head of Engineering at Flick Electric, will be talking about DORA to measure and improve DevOps performance.

## Q&A

Flick Electric shakes up the electricity market in Aotearoa and makes things better for Kiwis, their wallets, and the planet. They're putting pressure on the Electricity Authority and Government to fix the broken market.

A Q&A will be organised during the event to learn more about the company, feel free to ask some questions before the event.

Come join us to learn more about Flick Electric.

## Schedule

Doors open at 4:50pm and the first talk will start at 5:10pm.

- Advanced SQL Queries in Rails.
- Measure and improve DevOps performance with DORA.
- Flick Electric Q&A
- Job Openings
- Food + Drinks

## Ruby on Rails Companies series

This event is part of a new short Meetup series called "Ruby on Rails Companies" where each event highlights one Wellingtonian company using Ruby on Rails as part of their stack.

We're always looking to learn more about companies using Rails. Feel free to contact us to organise one event with your company.
