In March the team at Paloma (formerly Dovetail) will host us at their office to learn about how they use Rails in the ventures they're involved with.

Paloma is a product agency, venture studio and VC firm that works with established and early-stage tech companies to build world-class products and new startups. They are best known for building the consumer-facing tech behind several unicorns, including Afterpay.

Paloma currently use Rails on ChemCloud and have previously used it on Afterpay, Runn and Marmalade

## Talks

**"Bundler from inside out"**, Thong Kuah, Staff Engineer at GitLab, will take us through some learnings from working with the Bundler source code.

**"How we used Rails to build a marketplace in record time"**, Saurabh Bhatia, Engineering Manager at Paloma.

About the speaker: Saurabh had 16 years of Rails experience and now spends his time as an engineer trying this best to be a manager.

About the talk: The time's October 2021. The brief: Build and Launch a marketplace with features like real time chat before Christmas with just 2 people and limited venture budget?
The Answer: Rails 7 enters the room and 2 months later, we were ready to be launched to the world. This is a short story about that.

## About Wellrailed

Wellrailed is a Wellington based Rails meetup which has been part of the Rails community for over 10 years! Wellington is home to many companies who use Ruby on Rails so we're doing bi-monthly meetups hosted by a different company.

This is your chance to learn some stuff, network with your peers and learn more about how various local companies use Rails!

If you're interested in doing a talk or hosting an event, please get in touch with co-organisers Jordan Crawford or AJ Esler via Meetup.com or via the RubyNZ Slack.
