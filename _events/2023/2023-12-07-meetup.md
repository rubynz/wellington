Just like last year, our December meetup is a casual social catch-up.
Everyone is welcome to come along and share a drink, some food and good chats.

We've booked a table for 10 people at Bin44 - we can squeeze in a few more people and if it's popular I'll see if the booking can be increased.

We'll update the meetup with instructions on where to find us on the night.

There is no sponsor and no bar tab so you'll need to pay your own way.

Depending on numbers we may do another (very casual!) Ruby/Rails/computing quiz at some point of the night!

## Everyone is included

Despite being hosted at a bar there's absolutely no pressure to drink alcohol and we'll make sure the venue will have many non-alcoholic beverages available. The venue will also have vegan, vegetarian and gluten free food items available.

## There are no talks scheduled

But we'd love to chat about what talks you'd like to hear (or present!) in 2024.

## About Wellrailed

Wellrailed is a Wellington based Rails meetup which has been part of the Rails community for over 10 years! Wellington is home to many companies who use Ruby on Rails so we're doing bi-monthly meetups hosted by a different company.

This is your chance to learn some stuff, network with your peers and learn more about how various local companies use Rails!

If you're interested in doing a talk or hosting an event, please get in touch with co-organisers Jordan Crawford or AJ Esler via Meetup.com or via the RubyNZ Slack.

## Code of Conduct

At Wellrailed events we expect attendees to follow the Ruby NZ code of conduct - see ruby.nz/code-of-conduct
