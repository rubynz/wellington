Wellrailed meetups are back for 2024! As usual, we'll listen to some talks then share some pizza and a drink together!

Everyone is welcome - whether you're just starting out or a seasoned professional! While we're primarily a Ruby/Rails meetup, many of the topics will be applicable to all tech stacks.

**Sponsors:** A massive thank you to [Flick Electric](https://www.flickelectric.co.nz/) (and their parent company, Z Energy) who are letting us use their office space and [Axos Systems](https://www.axos.systems/) for sponsoring the cost of pizza & drinks.

*Flick Electric* shakes up the electricity market in Aotearoa and makes things better for Kiwis, their wallets, and the planet. They're putting pressure on the Electricity Authority and Government to fix the broken market.

*Axos Systems* provides a targeted set of cloud-based services that help energy market participants operate effectively and efficiently in a complex and changing world. The energy sector in NZ processes well over 3 billion pieces of market reconciliation information every month.

**Talks**

- **Landscaping Tricks: Safely adapting code to develop insight** — Matthew Gray

Applied techniques for applying Martin Fowler's preparatory refactoring through stacked pull requests. Commentary on improving your design by thinking about tests differently, and some hot takes on VCR.

- **Safely deploying a large release** — Monty Anderson

Talking about a large release, and how they made it safe with their testing approach, observability setup, use of flagging, and deployment/rollback strategies.

**About Wellrailed:** Wellrailed is a Wellington based Rails meetup which has been part of the Rails community for over 10 years! Wellington is home to many companies who use Ruby on Rails so we're doing bi-monthly meetups hosted by a different company. If you're interested in doing a talk or hosting an event, please get in touch with co-organisers Jordan Crawford or AJ Esler via Meetup.com or via the RubyNZ Slack.
