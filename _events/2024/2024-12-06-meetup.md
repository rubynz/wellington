Just like last year, our December meetup is a casual social catch-up. Everyone is welcome to come along and share a drink, some food and good chats.

There is no sponsor and no bar tab so you'll need to pay your own way.

**We've booked a table at Panhead Tory St from 5pm.** Please RSVP if you'll be coming (or remove your RSVP so we can reduce the booking). A RSVP limit has been added but if we blow past this we'll update the booking and increase the limit.

**Everyone is included:** Despite being hosted at a bar there's absolutely no pressure to drink alcohol and we'll make sure the venue will have many non-alcoholic beverages available. The venue will also have vegan, vegetarian and gluten free food items available.

**There are no talks scheduled** — but we'd love to chat about what talks you'd like to hear (or present!) in 2025.

**About Ruby Wellington:** Ruby Wellington (formally known as Wellrailed, Wellington-Rails) is a Wellington, New Zealand based tech meetup which has been part of the community for well over 10 years! Wellington is home to many companies who use Ruby so we're doing bi-monthly meetups hosted by a different company. If you're interested in doing a talk or hosting an event, please get in touch with co-organisers Jordan Crawford or AJ Esler via Meetup.com or via the RubyNZ Slack.

**Code of Conduct:** At Ruby Wellington events we expect attendees to follow the Ruby NZ code of conduct - see [ruby.nz/code-of-conduct](https://ruby.nz/code-of-conduct/)
