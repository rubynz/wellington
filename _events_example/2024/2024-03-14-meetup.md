Two sessions this month, hosted by Flick Electric at the Z Energy offices. Mathew Hartley (Senior Engineer at AMP) talks about intentionally modularising Rails applications using packwerk and packs — keeping a codebase clean and modular as it grows without departing from the majestic monolith. Then everyone gets a few minutes to evangelise their favourite tool that makes their development experience more pleasant.
