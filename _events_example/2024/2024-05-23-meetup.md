Two talks this month, hosted by Abletech. Paul Prestidge (Engineering Team Lead at Lyssna) makes the case for Rails API + React SPA and covers specific tooling around OpenAPI, @openapi-codegen/typescript, and react-query to bridge the gap with type safety. Ross Jourdain (Solution Architect at Abletech) demystifies DNS — covering address resolution, common developer tasks, and useful commands.
