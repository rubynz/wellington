title: Wellrailed July - The Security Special

A security-themed meetup hosted by PaperKite. Jim Rush (Security Consultant) delivers a crash course on the OWASP Top Ten for developers, including real world examples and exploitation techniques. Nick Malcolm (Security at GitLab) then runs a hands-on workshop using OWASP Juice Shop — an intentionally vulnerable web app — suitable for all skill levels.
