Two talks this month, hosted by Flick Electric at the Z Energy offices. Matthew Gray presents "Landscaping Tricks" — applied techniques for preparatory refactoring through stacked pull requests, with commentary on improving design through testing and some hot takes on VCR. Monty Anderson talks about safely deploying a large release using their testing approach, observability setup, feature flagging, and deployment/rollback strategies.
