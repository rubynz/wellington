Two talks this month, hosted by PaperKite. Toh Jagraphob explores the explosion of the Data Engineering ecosystem and what Ruby developers can do to take advantage of this data age. Jordan Crawford shares the woes of accessing personal financial data in New Zealand, walking through the current landscape of solutions, several DIY iterations, and some optimism for the open banking future.
