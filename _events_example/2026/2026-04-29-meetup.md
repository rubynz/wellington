title: April - UUIDs and levelling up

Two talks this month. Gordon Chan (Staff Software Engineer at Buildkite) traces the evolution of database identifiers from sequential integers to UUIDv7, drawing from Buildkite's journey including war stories about negative IDs and performance pitfalls. Thong Kuah (Principal Engineer at GitLab) shares a practical framework for engineers looking to move from Senior to Staff, covering how to demonstrate and increase your impact.
