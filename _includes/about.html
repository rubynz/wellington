History

The meetup started with a focus on the Ruby on Rails framework in 2006 under the name Wellrailed (meaning Wellington-Rails). In the early days, the group discussed through a Google Group. Eventually the group moved to Meetup.com.

In 2024, the meetup was renamed to Ruby Wellington to reflect it's broad reach beyond just the Rails framework.

Schedule

The meetup currently runs every two months with a bit of a break over summer - typically March, May, July, September and November.

Meetup Subscription

A massive thank you to Ruby NZ who covers the cost of our Meetup.com organiser subscription - this allows us to continue to focus on hosting events.

Getting involved

If you're interested in hosting a meetup or doing a talk please get in touch with co-organisers Jordan Crawford via Meetup.com or via the Ruby Oceania Slack (we're in the #meetup-wellington channel).
